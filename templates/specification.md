# Specification: [initiative]

## Objective

State the user problem and intended outcome from the approved product direction.

## Users and key scenarios

Describe the relevant users and the situations the change must support.

## Requirements and acceptance criteria

Use observable, testable statements. Keep desired behavior separate from implementation choices.

## Constraints and exclusions

List applicable product, privacy, security, accessibility, operational, and compatibility constraints; identify what is not included.

## Assumptions and unresolved questions

Mark assumptions explicitly. Record unresolved questions with an owner and whether they block implementation.

## Success measures

Define available measures and their source. Do not invent baselines or target values.

## Approval and status

Status, approver, date, and link to the authoritative decision record if applicable.
